# AMM Task 28-20-00-200-001 — Fuel System Inspection and Leak Check

## ATA Chapter 28 — Fuel

### 1. Purpose
Inspection of fuel tanks, lines, valves, and pumps. Includes leak detection, fuel quantity indicating system check, and fuel filter replacement.

### 2. Torque Specifications

| Component | Torque Value | Unit | Tolerance |
|-----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Fuel filter housing bolt | 25 | ft-lbs | ±3 |
| Fuel line B-nut (3/8") | 135 | in-lbs | ±10 |
| Fuel line B-nut (1/2") | 200 | in-lbs | ±15 |
| Fuel pump mounting bolt | 45 | ft-lbs | ±5 |
| Fuel valve actuator bolt | 30 | in-lbs | ±3 |
| Sump drain valve | 60 | in-lbs | ±5 |
| Fuel quantity probe mount | 20 | in-lbs | ±2 |

### 3. Fuel Tank Inspection

**Step 1:** Ensure tanks are properly defueled and purged per safety procedures.

**Step 2:** Inspect tank interior for:
- Corrosion (especially at riveted joints and sealant interfaces)
- Sealant deterioration or disbond
- Foreign object debris
- Water contamination evidence (staining, biological growth)

**Step 3:** Check all tank access panels:
- Gasket condition (replace if compressed beyond 75% of original thickness)
- Fastener condition and torque

### 4. Fuel Line Inspection

**Step 1:** Inspect all rigid fuel lines for:
- Corrosion or pitting
- Chafing from adjacent structure (min clearance: 0.25 in)
- Dents exceeding 20% of tube diameter

**Step 2:** Inspect all flexible fuel hoses for:
- Age (max service life: 5 years or per manufacturer)
- Cracking, bulging, or soft spots
- Chafing or abrasion
- Fitting security

**Step 3:** Pressure test fuel system: 15 PSI for 30 minutes. No drop = pass.

### 5. Fuel Filter Replacement

**Step 1:** Close fuel shutoff valves upstream and downstream.

**Step 2:** Place drip pan under filter housing. Remove housing bolts.

**Step 3:** Remove old filter element. Inspect for contamination:
- If metallic particles found → sample for analysis, inspect fuel pump
- If biological growth found → treat tank per AMM 28-20-00-600-001

**Step 4:** Install new filter element (verify P/N per IPC).

**Step 5:** Reinstall housing. Torque bolts to 25 ft-lbs ±3 in star pattern.

**Step 6:** Open fuel valves. Check for leaks at operating pressure.

### 6. Cautions
- **DANGER:** Fuel vapors are explosive. No ignition sources within 50 feet. Ground aircraft and all equipment before beginning work.
- **WARNING:** Any fuel leak, regardless of size, is an airworthiness concern. Do not dispatch aircraft with known fuel leaks.
- **CAUTION:** Fuel system sealant requires 24-hour cure time at 77°F before pressure testing.
