# AMM Task 27-00-00-200-001 — Flight Control System Rigging and Inspection

## ATA Chapter 27 — Flight Controls

### 1. Purpose
Inspection and rigging verification of primary and secondary flight control systems including ailerons, elevators, rudder, flaps, and spoilers.

### 2. Torque Specifications

| Component | Torque Value | Unit | Tolerance |
|-----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Control rod end bearing | 60 | in-lbs | ±5 |
| Bellcrank pivot bolt | 100 | in-lbs | ±10 |
| Cable turnbuckle locknut | 40 | in-lbs | ±5 |
| Actuator attach bolt | 75 | ft-lbs | ±5 |
| Hinge pin nut | 25 | ft-lbs | ±3 |
| Trim tab actuator mount | 35 | in-lbs | ±3 |
| Push-pull rod clevis pin | 50 | in-lbs | ±5 |

### 3. Control Surface Travel Limits

| Surface | Full Up/Left | Neutral | Full Down/Right |
|---------|-------------|---------|----------------|
| Aileron | 25° ±1° | 0° ±0.5° | 15° ±1° |
| Elevator | 30° ±1° | 0° ±0.5° | 20° ±1° |
| Rudder | 27° ±1° left | 0° ±0.5° | 27° ±1° right |
| Flaps (full) | — | 0° | 40° ±1° |
| Spoiler | — | 0° (flush) | 60° ±2° |

### 4. Inspection Procedure

**Step 1:** Verify hydraulic system depressurized and control locks installed before beginning inspection.

**Step 2:** Inspect all control cables for:
- Broken wires (max 3 in any one lay length)
- Corrosion or kinking
- Cable tension: 40 lbs ±5 at 70°F ambient (correct for temperature per rigging chart)

**Step 3:** Inspect all rod ends and bearings for:
- Free play exceeding 0.005 in
- Corrosion or binding
- Safety wire integrity

**Step 4:** Verify control surface travel using protractor/inclinometer per limits in Section 3.

**Step 5:** Check control surface balance weights:
- Visually inspect for cracks at attachment
- Verify no paint buildup altering balance
- If balance weight is damaged → ground aircraft, refer to SRM 27-00-00-300-001

**Step 6:** Inspect all bellcranks, idler pulleys, and fairleads for wear and security.

### 5. Rigging Adjustment

**Step 1:** Set control column to neutral. Verify control surface at 0° ±0.5°.

**Step 2:** Adjust cable tension using turnbuckles. Torque locknuts to 40 in-lbs ±5.

**Step 3:** Verify full travel in both directions per limits table.

**Step 4:** Safety wire all turnbuckles per AS567 using 0.032 in safety wire.

### 6. Cautions
- **WARNING:** Flight control system is critical to flight safety. All work must be inspected by a second qualified person and signed off before aircraft release.
- **WARNING:** Never leave tools or FOD in the control cable routing area. Perform tool inventory before closing panels.
- **CAUTION:** Cable tension varies with temperature. Always record ambient temperature when measuring.
