# AMM Task 32-10-00-200-001 — Main Landing Gear Inspection

## ATA Chapter 32 — Landing Gear

### 1. Purpose
Scheduled inspection of main landing gear (MLG) assembly including strut, actuators, brakes, tires, and associated hydraulic components.

### 2. Torque Specifications

| Component | Torque Value | Unit | Tolerance |
|-----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| MLG trunnion pin nut | 300 | ft-lbs | ±25 |
| Brake assembly bolts | 50 | ft-lbs | ±5 |
| Hydraulic fitting B-nuts | 90 | in-lbs | ±10 |
| Wheel axle nut | 250 | ft-lbs | ±20 |
| Side brace link bolt | 150 | ft-lbs | ±15 |
| Downlock spring pin | 35 | in-lbs | ±3 |
| Tire inflation valve | 15 | in-lbs | ±2 |

### 3. Strut Inspection

**Step 1:** Check strut extension. Normal: 4.5–6.0 inches with aircraft on jacks. Record measurement.

**Step 2:** Check strut for:
- Hydraulic leaks at seals (trace weep acceptable, drip = replace seal)
- Chrome pitting or corrosion on piston
- Scoring exceeding 0.003 in depth → replace strut per SRM 32-10-00-300-001

**Step 3:** Service strut nitrogen pressure:
- Target: 1800 PSI (empty weight) / 2400 PSI (max taxi weight)
- If pressure below 1500 PSI, inspect for seal leaks

**Step 4:** Inspect torque links for wear at pivot bushings. Max play: 0.010 in.

### 4. Brake Inspection

**Step 1:** Measure brake wear indicators. Minimum remaining: 0.1 in pad thickness.

**Step 2:** Check brake discs for:
- Cracks exceeding 1.5 in length
- Heat discoloration beyond limits (refer to CMM 32-40-00)
- Warping: max 0.015 in runout

**Step 3:** Check hydraulic brake lines for chafing, leaks, and security.

### 5. Tire Inspection

**Step 1:** Check tire pressure: 200 PSI ±10 (main gear), 150 PSI ±10 (nose gear).

**Step 2:** Inspect tread depth. Minimum: 1/32 in remaining at any point.

**Step 3:** Check for:
- Sidewall cuts exposing cord
- Flat spots from locked-brake landing
- Foreign object damage
- Bead area damage

### 6. Cautions
- **WARNING:** Main landing gear retraction test must only be performed with aircraft on approved jacks and gear pins removed. Verify hydraulic pressure before cycling.
- **CAUTION:** Do not exceed tire pressure limits. Over-inflation can cause explosive failure.
- **CAUTION:** Brake discs exceeding crack limits are non-airworthy. Aircraft must not be dispatched.
