# AMM Task 72-00-00-200-001 — Engine Bearing Inspection and Replacement

## ATA Chapter 72 — Engine

### 1. Purpose
This task card covers the inspection, measurement, and replacement procedures for main engine bearings (No. 1 through No. 5) on CFM56-series engines.

### 2. Required Tools and Equipment
- Torque wrench (calibrated, range 10–100 in-lbs)
- Bearing puller set (P/N JT8D-BP-001)
- Dial indicator (0.0001 in resolution)
- Borescope (min 4mm diameter)
- Safety wire pliers

### 3. Torque Specifications

| Component | Torque Value | Unit | Tolerance |
|-----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| No. 1 bearing B-nut | 35 | in-lbs | ±3 |
| No. 2 bearing B-nut | 35 | in-lbs | ±3 |
| No. 3 bearing B-nut | 40 | in-lbs | ±3 |
| No. 4 bearing B-nut | 30 | in-lbs | ±3 |
| No. 5 bearing B-nut | 35 | in-lbs | ±3 |
| Bearing housing bolts | 55 | in-lbs | ±5 |
| Oil seal retainer | 25 | in-lbs | ±2 |
| Accessory gearbox mount | 45 | ft-lbs | ±5 |

### 4. Inspection Procedure

**Step 1:** Remove engine cowling per AMM 71-00-00-100-001.

**Step 2:** Disconnect oil supply and scavenge lines at the bearing housing. Cap all open lines immediately.

**Step 3:** Remove the bearing housing bolts (6 each). Torque on reinstallation: 55 in-lbs ±5.

**Step 4:** Inspect bearing for:
- Scoring or spalling on rolling elements
- Discoloration indicating overheating (blue/purple tint)
- Cage wear or deformation
- Oil contamination or metallic debris

**Step 5:** Measure bearing radial play using dial indicator:
- Acceptable range: 0.002–0.005 in
- If radial play exceeds 0.005 in, replace bearing

**Step 6:** Inspect B-nut threads for damage or cross-threading.

**Step 7:** If bearing is serviceable, reinstall with new O-ring (P/N MS29513-012) and safety wire.

### 5. Replacement Procedure

**Step 1:** Using bearing puller, remove old bearing. Do not use impact tools.

**Step 2:** Inspect bearing seat for scoring or corrosion. If seat is damaged, refer to SRM 72-00-00-300-001.

**Step 3:** Install new bearing (verify P/N matches IPC). Apply thin coat of MIL-PRF-23699 oil to bearing surfaces.

**Step 4:** Install new O-ring (P/N MS29513-012) on B-nut. Lubricate with MIL-PRF-83282 hydraulic fluid.

**Step 5:** Torque B-nut to specification per table in Section 3.

**Step 6:** Safety wire per AS567 standard.

**Step 7:** Reconnect oil lines. Torque oil line B-nuts to 90 in-lbs ±5.

### 6. Ops Check
After reassembly, perform ground run per EMM 72-00-00-710-001:
- Monitor oil pressure and temperature for 5 minutes at idle
- Check for leaks at all disturbed connections
- Oil pressure must be 40–55 PSI at idle
- Oil temperature must stabilize below 250°F

### 7. Cautions
- **CAUTION:** Never reuse O-rings on B-nut fittings. Always install new.
- **CAUTION:** Bearing must be handled with clean, lint-free gloves. Skin oils cause premature failure.
- **WARNING:** If metallic debris is found in oil, ground aircraft and perform full oil system inspection per AMM 79-00-00-200-001.
